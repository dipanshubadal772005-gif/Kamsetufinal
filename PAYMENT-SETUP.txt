KAMSETU POST-COMPLETION PAYMENT (PROTOTYPE)

Flow:
1. Provider completes the job.
2. Customer opens My Bookings.
3. A completed booking shows "Pay Provider".
4. Customer selects UPI or Cash.
5. For UPI, customer enters the transaction/reference ID after paying through their chosen UPI app.
6. KAMSETU records the booking as paid in MongoDB.
7. The Pay Provider button changes to Payment Completed.

Backend endpoints:
PATCH /api/bookings/:id/request-payment
PATCH /api/bookings/:id/payment

Important:
This is a payment-status/confirmation flow for the prototype. It does NOT process card/bank/UPI money itself.
A real payment gateway (such as Razorpay) is required for automated payment processing and verification.
